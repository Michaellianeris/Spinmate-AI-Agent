Fixed in version 2.0

  • F1 · Static anisotropy added through the AC term. ku() in current.py returned the static K when flag2 was off, and solver.py added it again along u_ani_AC. The total, 4 Ku/(mu0 Ms) per sublattice, is right for sublattices of Ms/2 and gives the AFM/FM boundary |Ku| = 2|A0|/l^2, but it broke when u_ani_AC differs from u_ani or Ku(t) is on. Fixed: K = 4 Ku/(mu0 Ms^2) in one term, ku() returns only the AC part.
  • F4 · Ku(t) amplitude normalised with g0. ku() divided Ku_Amp by g0 Ms^2 instead of mu0 Ms^2, so Ku(t) was 1.76e11 times too weak. Fixed.
  • F2 · RK4 weights and stages. RK4() used 0.166 instead of 1/6 and fed the m1 slopes into the m2 stages. Fixed; Heun stays the default.
  • F3 · Thermal field direction. The thermal field was Temp_Ampl times a unit vector from np.random.rand(3), with only positive components. Fixed: a Gaussian field with standard deviation Temp_Ampl per component, as run.py defines it.
