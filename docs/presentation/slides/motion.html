A current moves the skyrmion. Under spin-orbit torque from a heavy metal it runs at 116 metres per second, almost at right angles to the current, the skyrmion Hall effect. Under spin-transfer torque with beta equal to alpha it drifts exactly with the electrons. The card compares each run with the Thiele equation.
