The same engine sends spin waves from an antenna. A sine at 80 gigahertz gives waves of 49.3 nanometres, against 49.6 from theory. A broadband sinc pulse excites every wave at once, and the Fourier transform in space and time lies on the analytic dispersion within one percent. The arrows use the same style as the skyrmions; the colour is the phase of the precession, enlarged so it can be seen.
