With demag equal to full, mm2d computes the stray field of the whole film by FFT with the Newell tensor. A platelet reproduces the Aharoni demagnetising factor to one part in a million, and a Néel wall costs the expected extra stray-field energy within half a percent. That gives dipolar bubbles without any DMI, here forty-seven nanometres at fifty millitesla, and maze domains in a twenty-nanometre film from a random start, which the local thin-film field cannot produce.
