The guide

How to use the app; built-in answers offline

On Auto, every request is scored over the persona’s models

score = capability + bonus − λ(persona, request) × log₁₀(price / cheapest) − shortfall

The strongest model wins unless a cheaper one is clearly enough. Every answer shows the model, the reason and the cost in €, at the daily ECB rate.

6

A persona is a level of model, not a fixed model. The router scores all models of that level from every provider with a key: Anthropic, OpenAI and Gemini. λ is largest for Hermes and smallest for Zeus, and it shrinks for demanding requests: Deep mode, sweeps, comparisons, reports. If a call fails, the next model by score is used. A model can also be pinned per persona in Settings.
