mm2d is SpinMate's own finite-difference solver, written in NumPy, for a ferromagnetic film one cell thick. It relaxes a seed to the nearest stable texture, here a Néel skyrmion of radius 14.4 nanometres and a skyrmionium with rings at 8.5 and 34.4 nanometres, in a few seconds on the CPU. It is checked against analytic wall energies and widths, the Kittel frequency and energy conservation. The run card shows the texture as a tilted 3D view and a top view.
