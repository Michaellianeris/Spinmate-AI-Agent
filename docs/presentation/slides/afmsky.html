The two-sublattice heart of SpinMate now meets micromagnetics. In a synthetic antiferromagnet, two ferromagnetic layers coupled antiparallel through a spacer as in Pt/Co/Ru multilayers, the skyrmions of the two layers have opposite topological charge. Their Magnus forces cancel, so under spin-orbit torque the pair moves straight along the current at 131 metres per second, six times faster than a single layer, which is deflected by eighty degrees.
