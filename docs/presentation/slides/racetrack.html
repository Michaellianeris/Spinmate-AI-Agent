The film can now take any shape: a disk, a strip, a racetrack, or a PNG dropped on the chat, where the dark pixels become the film. Its edges carry the boundary condition of the DMI, so the magnetisation tilts there as theory predicts, within one percent, and the tilted edges push back on a skyrmion. Under a spin-orbit current the edges cancel the skyrmion Hall effect, and the skyrmion runs along the track at sixty-eight metres per second, much faster than the fourteen and a half it would reach in an open film.
